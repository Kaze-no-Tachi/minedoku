How to play
  1. Exactly one mine in every row.
  2. Exactly one mine in every column.
  3. Exactly one mine in every colour.
  4. No two mines may touch, not even at a corner.

Tap a cell once to mark it as clear (X), twice to place a mine, three times to empty it. Press and hold to drop a mine straight away. Placing a mine automatically X's out everything it rules out.

Every board has exactly one solution, so you never have to guess.

Rules, generator and hints run on the same Dart engine as the Flutter app, compiled to JavaScript.
